Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 October 2026

ProtonMob Teknoloji Anonim Şirketi (“ProtonMob”, “we”), Maslak Mah. AOS 55. Sk. 42 Maslak A Blok No:2/25, Sarıyer, İstanbul, Türkiye, makes mobile apps. Each app has its own privacy policy, linked from its App Store page; this page covers ProtonMob Studio, our internal tool for running those apps. Questions, and requests to see or delete data: erdi@protonmob.com.

Who uses Studio

Studio is not offered to the public. Only ProtonMob staff sign in to it, with their Google account, and it connects only to accounts that ProtonMob owns: our apps’ TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Facebook accounts, our Meta and Apple Search Ads advertising accounts, and App Store Connect.

What Studio does with connected accounts

  • TikTok: with the account’s permission, Studio sends a video and its caption to the account’s TikTok inbox as a draft; a person then reviews and posts it in the TikTok app. It reads the account’s basic profile (name, avatar) to show which account is connected.
  • YouTube: with the channel’s permission, Studio uploads a video with its title and description to that channel, and reads the channel’s name and its videos’ public statistics.
  • Instagram and Facebook: Studio publishes posts to our Pages and Instagram accounts and reads their follower counts, reach and post statistics.
  • Meta and Apple Search Ads: Studio reads our campaigns, spend, installs and keywords, and can pause or resume our own campaigns.

What we keep, and for how long

  • Access tokens the platforms issue when an account is connected are kept encrypted in Google Cloud Secret Manager, used only by Studio’s server, and deleted when the account is disconnected. They are never shown in Studio or sent anywhere else.
  • Videos and images uploaded for a post are kept in a private Google Cloud Storage bucket and deleted automatically after 7 days.
  • Statistics are read when a Studio page is opened and are not stored, except in the monthly analyses we keep about our own apps.

Studio does not read anyone else’s accounts, messages or personal data, does not sell or share data, and does not use it for advertising profiles.

Google user data

Studio’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is used only to upload our videos and show our channels’ statistics inside Studio. Studio uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time from Google security settings.

Disconnecting

An account can be disconnected in Studio or from the platform’s own settings (TikTok: Settings → Security → Manage app permissions; Google: the link above; Meta: Business Settings). When an account is disconnected we delete its token; write to us to have anything else deleted.

Changes

If this policy changes we update the date at the top of this page.
