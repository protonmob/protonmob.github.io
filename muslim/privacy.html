Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 October 2026

This page explains what Muslim Car Play Widgets collects, who it reaches, and what you can switch off. In this policy “we” means Muslim Car Play Widgets. For privacy questions — including a request to see or delete your data — write to erdi@protonmob.com.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or date of birth. There is no sign-up screen and no password to choose. We do not sell personal data, and we do not track you across other companies’ apps and websites: the app never asks for the iOS permission to do so, and never reads your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA).

How you are identified

On first launch the app generates a random identifier and stores it in your iCloud Keychain, in a keychain group shared by our apps, so it survives reinstalling the app and moving to a new Apple device with the same Apple Account, and you are counted once rather than twice. Firebase Authentication keeps an account for the app that is derived from that identifier — no email address of yours is involved — and the identifier is sent with requests to our server. That identifier is all we know you by: no name, no email address, no password.

Our server keeps a record for that identifier: the app’s language and region, whether you have Pro, how many free uses you have had, and one entry per App Store install (device model, iOS version, and whether it was a reinstall). That is what lets free uses and a subscription follow you to a new phone.

Analytics

The app reports what happens in it to Google Analytics for Firebase and to Amplitude. The events are about actions, not content: which screens you open, which onboarding step you reached, which settings you change, and whether you started a subscription. They carry the device model, OS version, app version, language and approximate region (derived from the network, not from GPS), and the random identifier above.

Screen recording (session replay)

To understand where the app confuses people, we use Amplitude Session Replay, which records a video-like reconstruction of the screens you see and the taps you make in this app. Text you type is masked before it leaves your device. Recordings are attached to the random identifier above, not to your name.

Crash and performance reports

Firebase Crashlytics receives a report when the app crashes, and when it recovers from an error. Firebase Performance Monitoring measures how long screens and network requests take. Both include the device model, OS version, app version and the random identifier.

App integrity and configuration

Firebase App Check uses Apple’s App Attest and DeviceCheck to confirm that requests to our server come from a genuine copy of the app; it does not identify you. Firebase Remote Config downloads settings that let us change how the app behaves without an update. Our server logic runs on Cloud Functions for Firebase.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app registers with Apple Push Notification service and stores the resulting Firebase Cloud Messaging token on our server, so that a notification can reach this device. Turning notifications off in iOS Settings stops them.

Advertising measurement

The app includes the Meta (Facebook) SDK, so that if we advertise on Facebook or Instagram we can tell whether those ads led to installs and subscriptions. It sends the same action events as our analytics (such as “app opened”, “onboarding finished”, “subscription started”), with device and app information, the random identifier above and Meta’s own anonymous app id. The app does not ask for permission to track, advertising-identifier collection is switched off, and automatic event logging is switched off, so Meta never receives your IDFA and the events are not used to follow you across other companies’ apps.

Subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. Subscription status is managed for us by RevenueCat, Inc., which receives the purchase record from Apple and the random identifier above — not your Apple Account. So that a purchase or renewal is counted correctly, RevenueCat forwards it to Firebase Analytics, Amplitude and Meta, together with the ids each of them already uses for this install.

If you redeem a promo code, the code and the random identifier are stored so the unlock can be checked later.

When you contact us

A message sent from the app’s Support screen, or by email, is stored so that we can answer it. From the app it carries what you wrote, the random identifier, the app version, device model, iOS version and language, so a reply does not have to begin by asking, and a screenshot if you choose to attach one. If you give an email address, that is stored too. A copy of each new message is forwarded to our team’s private chat (Telegram) so that a person sees it promptly, and our reply reaches you as a notification in the app.

Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to ask for a copy of your data, to have it corrected, or to have it deleted. Because we hold no name or email address, we cannot look you up by either — a request has to carry the app’s identifier for you, which the Support screen in the app sends along automatically. You can also write to erdi@protonmob.com. We respond within 30 days.

Under Turkish law (KVKK) the data controller (veri sorumlusu) is Muslim Car Play Widgets, reachable at the email address above.

If you are in the EU or the UK

The legal bases we rely on under the GDPR are:

  • Performance of a contract — the identifier, backend requests and subscription status, without which the app cannot provide what you use it for.
  • Legitimate interests — crash, performance and integrity checks, to keep the app working and secure; product analytics and session replay, to understand which parts of the app are used and fix what does not work; and aggregated advertising measurement, to know which of our campaigns bring people to the app. None of this is used to track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and no advertising identifier (IDFA) is collected.

Your right to object. You can object to any processing based on legitimate interests at any time, without giving a reason. Turning off Settings → Share usage analytics in the app stops analytics, session replay and advertising measurement on that device immediately. To object to anything else, or to have what we hold erased, use Settings → Contact support → Delete my data or write to the address above; we will confirm when it is done.

Most of our processors are based in the United States, so data is transferred outside the EEA under their standard contractual clauses. You have the right to complain to your local supervisory authority.

Children

Muslim Car Play Widgets is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and, where the change affects what is collected, say so in the app.
