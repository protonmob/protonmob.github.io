The fastest route is the Support screen inside the app: it attaches the app version and the identifier we need to look into a purchase or a sync problem, so you do not have to type any of it.

Subscriptions and purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. To cancel or change a subscription, open Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions on your iPhone. To bring a purchase to a new phone, use Restore purchases in the app's Settings. Refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
